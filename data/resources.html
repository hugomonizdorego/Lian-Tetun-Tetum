Instalasaun / Installation

sudo apt install ./eduka-language-pack-tet_0.2.0_all.deb
eduka-tet demo
eduka-tet enable --user

Sai husi sesaun no tama fali. / Log out and sign in again.

Run enable as your normal user, without sudo. Activation detects the desktop; Plasma and GNOME/GDM have specific backends. Sign out and sign in again. Some Wayland sessions require login-manager configuration. See INSTALL-ID.md.

Atu dezativa / To undo:

eduka-tet disable --user
